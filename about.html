About Kampala Youth Employment Connect

Kampala Youth Employment Connect is a website designed to help young people in Kampala access information about employment opportunities, internships, skills training and career development.

The website is intended for university and college graduates, secondary school leavers, vocational graduates and other young people looking for employment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make employment information easier to access and help young people discover opportunities that can improve their career prospects.

Our Vission

We aim to contribute to a community where young people in Kampala can access useful information, develop their skills and make informed career decisions.
