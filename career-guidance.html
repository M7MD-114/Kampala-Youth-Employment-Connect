Career Guidance and Advice

Choosing a career and looking for employment can be difficult, especially for young people who are just starting their professional journey.

This page provides basic guidance to help young people in Kampala prepare for employment, make career decisions and understand the job application process.

How to Prepare a Good CV

  1. Include your full name and appropriate contact details.
  2. Write a short professional profile.
  3. List your education and qualifications.
  4. Include relevant skills and experience.
  5. Check spelling and grammar before sending your CV.
  6. Keep the information truthful and up to date.

Preparing for a Job Interview

  • Research the organisation before the interview.
  • Read the job description carefully.
  • Practise answering common interview questions.
  • Dress appropriately for the workplace.
  • Arrive on time and communicate confidently.
  • Ask polite questions when given the opportunity.

Choosing a Career Path

Think about your interests, strengths, education and the skills you would like to develop. You can also speak to teachers, career advisers and experienced professionals for guidance.
