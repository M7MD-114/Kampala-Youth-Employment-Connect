Information for Employers

Kampala Youth Employment Connect aims to create a link between employers and young people looking for employment opportunities in Kampala.

Employers can use this platform to learn how to connect with young job seekers, graduates and people searching for internship opportunities.

How Employers Can Support Young People

  • Provide fair employment opportunities for young people.
  • Offer internships and workplace learning opportunities.
  • Support mentorship and career development.
  • Provide clear job descriptions and application requirements.
  • Encourage equal opportunities for qualified applicants.

Benefits of Employing Young People

  • Access to fresh ideas and creativity.
  • Opportunities to develop future skilled workers.
  • Building a diverse and productive workforce.
  • Supporting youth development in the community.

Guidelines for Employers

Employers should provide accurate information about vacancies, qualifications, working conditions and application deadlines. They should also treat applicants fairly and protect the personal information they receive.
