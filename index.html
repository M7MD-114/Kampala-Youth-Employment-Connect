welcome to Kampala Youth Employment Connect

Finding employment can be difficult for many young people in Kampala. Some have completed university or college, while others have completed secondary school or vocational training but still struggle to find work.

Our website provides information about job opportunities, internships, skills training and career guidance to help young people take their next steps towards employment.
