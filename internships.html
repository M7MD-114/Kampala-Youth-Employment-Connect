internship Opportunities

Internships give young people an opportunity to gain practical work experience, learn from professionals and understand how workplaces operate.

Students, recent graduates and young job seekers in Kampala can use internships to develop skills and prepare for future employment.

Where to find Internships

  • BrighterMonday Uganda: Search for opportunities
  • United Nations Careers: Visit the official website
  • United Nation Careers Explore career and internship information

Benefits of Internships

  • Gaining practical work experience.
  • Learning new skills from experienced workers.
  • Building professional networks.
  • Improving communication and teamwork.
  • Understanding different career fields.

Tips Before Applying

  1. Prepare a professional CV.
  2. Write a clear application letter.
  3. Research the organisation offering the internship.
  4. Understand the internship requirements and duration.
  5. Never pay a person who promises to guarantee an internship.
