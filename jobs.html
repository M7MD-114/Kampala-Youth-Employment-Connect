Find Job Opportunities

Finding a job can be challenging for many young people in Kampala. Our website helps young people discover employment opportunities and connect with organisations offering jobs.

Where to Find Jobs

Visit these websites to explore available job opportunities:

  • BrighterMonday Uganda - Explore job vacancies from different employers.
  • Fuzu - Discover jobs and career development opportunities.
  • Uganda Public Service Commission - Find information about public service opportunities.

Tips for Finding a Job

  • Prepare a clear and updated CV.
  • Apply for jobs that match your skills and qualifications.
  • Prepare well for job interviews.
  • Check job advertisements regularly.
  • Improve your communication and computer skills.

Stay Safe When Searching for Jobs

Always check whether an employer is genuine before applying. Be careful with job advertisements that ask for money before offering employment. Never share private information with untrusted people.
